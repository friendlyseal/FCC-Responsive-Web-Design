Geoff Ramsey

He ain't dead he's just unemployed

  • Born 6/19/1975 in Mobile, Alabama
  • Served in Kuwait as a US Army photojournalist from 1993 to 1998
  • Meets Burnie Burns and Gus Sorola while working at an Austin TX tech support call center
  • Leaves the call center to start Roosterteeth, an online machinima production company
  • 2008 begins Achievement Hunter, originally producing achievement guides for various XBOX video games, eventually branching out to Lets Play content and live events
  • Amidst the 2020 pandemic, launches the magnum opus of his career, a comedy podcast F**kface along co-hosts Andrew Panton and Gavin Free
  • After the shutdown of Roosterteeth in May 2024, rebrands F**kface to the new banner Regulation Podcast while also launching an affiliated gaming channel Regulation Gameplay
